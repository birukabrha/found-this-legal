Support

Found This – lost & found app

Contact us

Email birukabrha@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Report a listing or user

Open the listing or chat, tap the menu, and choose Report. You can also block the user from the same menu. We review reports within 24 hours. For anything urgent or dangerous, contact your local police first.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Delete Account, then confirm.

Without the app: email birukabrha@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted: your name, profile photo, listings and their photos, chat photos, and notification tokens. Text messages you already sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, shown as from "Deleted user". Reports about abuse may be kept so we can deal with it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
