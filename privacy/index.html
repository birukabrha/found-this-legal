Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 November 2026

Found This ("the app", "we", "us") helps people who find lost items post them so the owners can find and contact them. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider, never in plain text.
  • Profile photo (optional).
  • Item listings: title, category, description, photos, the general location you type, the map point you choose, and the date found. Listings are public to everyone who uses the app.
  • Chat messages and photos you send to other users.
  • Reports you submit about listings or users, including the reason and any details you add.
  • Blocks: the list of users you have blocked.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token and device platform (iOS or Android), so we can send you chat notifications.
  • Settings such as whether chat notifications are on, and which version of the Terms you accepted and when.
  • Technical data such as IP address and request logs, which our hosting providers record when your device connects to them.
  • Advertising data: to show ads, Google AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier (where you allow it), IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See section 6.

The app does not track your device's GPS location. The only location stored is the place you choose on the map when you post an item. The app does not use analytics trackers.

3. How we use your information

  • To run the app: show listings, let people search them, and let users chat with each other.
  • To send you notifications about new messages (you can turn these off in Settings or in your device settings).
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms of Use, filter objectionable words, and suspend accounts that abuse the service.
  • To respond to you when you contact support.
  • To show ads, which help keep the app free.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. What other users can see

  • Your name and profile photo, on your listings and in chats.
  • Everything in your listings, including photos and the map location you chose. Avoid posting photos or descriptions that reveal personal details such as ID numbers, addresses or bank card numbers.
  • Messages you send are visible to the person you are chatting with.

5. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase – account sign-in, database and photo storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service – delivering notifications. A notification contains the sender's name and a preview of the message.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers – showing maps. Your device requests map images directly from them, which shares your IP address with them.
  • Google AdMob – showing ads. Google and its advertising partners receive the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

6. Advertising

Found This shows ads in some lists, for example between search results and on the home screen. Ads are clearly labelled "Sponsored". Ads are never shown inside chats or when you post an item.

  • Your content is not used for ads. We do not share your name, email, listings, photos or chat messages with advertisers.
  • Personalised ads: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ads are provided by third parties. We are not responsible for the content of ads or the websites they link to.

7. How long we keep data

  • Listings stay up until you delete them or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile name and photo, your listings and their photos, your chat photos, and your push tokens. The text messages you already sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, shown as from "Deleted user".
  • Reports may be kept after an account is deleted so we can deal with abuse.
  • Server logs and backups held by our providers are deleted on their normal schedules.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name and photo in Settings.
  • Delete listings at any time from My Listings.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing us (see the Support page).
  • Turn off notifications in Settings or your device settings.
  • Ad choices: change your ad consent in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices (where available), or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules limit each user to the data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Who can use the app

Found This is only for people 18 and older, because the app involves contacting and meeting people you do not know. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our providers may store and process data in countries other than yours. By using the app you understand that your information may be transferred to those countries.

12. Changes

If we make significant changes to this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
